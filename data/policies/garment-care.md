# Garment Care Guide
<!-- doc_id: CARE | updated: 2026-09-01 -->

## 1. Linen
Wash linen at 30 degrees on a gentle cycle and avoid the tumble dryer. Linen creases naturally; iron it while it is still slightly damp for the smoothest finish.

## 2. Cotton and organic cotton
Wash cotton at 30 degrees, inside out, with similar colours. Organic cotton may shrink slightly in a hot dryer, so use a low setting or dry it on a line.

## 3. Wool and merino
Hand wash wool in cold water with a wool detergent, or use the wool programme of your machine. Never wring it; dry it flat away from radiators to keep its shape.

## 4. Viscose and silk
Viscose and silk are delicate fibres. Hand wash cold or dry clean, do not wring, and iron on the reverse at a low temperature. Silk items marked dry clean only must not be washed.

## 5. Denim
Wash jeans inside out at 30 degrees and as rarely as possible to preserve the colour. Dark denim may release dye during the first washes.

## 6. Leather and suede
Wipe leather with a dry or slightly damp cloth and treat it with a leather conditioner. Brush suede gently, protect it with a suede spray and keep it away from water.
