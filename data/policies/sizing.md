# Size Guide
<!-- doc_id: SIZE | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. How to measure yourself
Use a soft tape measure over light clothing. Chest: around the fullest part. Waist: around the narrowest part, usually just above the belly button. Hip: around the fullest part of the hips. Foot length: from heel to longest toe while standing.

## 2. Fit types
Slim fit sits close to the body. Regular fit is true to size. Relaxed fit has extra room through the body. Oversized fit is intentionally loose; size down if you prefer a closer fit.

## 3. Between two sizes
If your measurements fall between two sizes, choose the larger size for slim fit items and the smaller size for relaxed or oversized items.

## 4. Size recommendations from height and weight
Recommendations based only on height and weight are an estimate. For the best result, measure your chest, waist and hips and compare them with the size chart of the product.
