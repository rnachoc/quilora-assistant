# Frequently Asked Questions
<!-- doc_id: FAQ | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. Gift cards
We do not sell gift cards at the moment.

## 2. Price changes after purchase
Prices can change during promotions. We do not refund price differences for orders that have already been placed.

## 3. Restocking sold-out items
Popular sizes are usually restocked within 3 to 6 weeks, but we cannot guarantee a date. Use the "notify me" button on the product page to receive an email when the size is back.

## 4. Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled only while it is still processing and has not been handed to the carrier. Cancellations are handled by our support team.

## 5. Physical stores
Quilora is an online-only store. We do not have physical shops or showrooms.
