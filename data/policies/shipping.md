# Shipping Policy
<!-- doc_id: SHP | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. Processing time
Orders placed before 14:00 (Spanish peninsula time) on business days are prepared and handed to the carrier the same or the next business day. Orders placed at weekends or on public holidays are processed the next business day.

## 2. Mainland Spain
Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days. Shipping costs 3.95 EUR and is free for orders of 50 EUR or more.

## 3. Balearic Islands
Delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Shipping costs 5.95 EUR and is free for orders of 80 EUR or more.

## 4. Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla
Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days. Shipping costs 9.95 EUR. Import taxes and customs fees may apply and are paid by the customer on delivery.

## 5. Portugal and the rest of the European Union
Portugal: 3 to 5 business days, 5.95 EUR, free from 80 EUR. Other EU countries: 4 to 7 business days, 9.95 EUR, free from 120 EUR. We do not ship outside the European Union.

## 6. Tracking your order
When your order ships you receive an email with the carrier name and tracking code. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier's website.

## 7. Delays and lost parcels
If your parcel has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact our support team. We open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or issue a refund.

## 8. Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can only be changed before the order ships. Changes must be handled by our support team; the assistant cannot modify orders.
