# Exchanges Policy
<!-- doc_id: EXC | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. Size and colour exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock availability. Each item can be exchanged once, and exchange shipping is free within mainland Spain.

## 2. Exchanging for a different product
We do not exchange one product for a different one. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.

## 3. How exchanges are processed
Exchanges are handled by our support team, who reserve the new size or colour and send you a label. The replacement ships as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier.
