# Customer Support
<!-- doc_id: SUP | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. Opening hours and response times
Our support team works Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 (Spanish peninsula time). We answer requests within 24 business hours.

## 2. When we transfer you to a person
The assistant transfers your conversation to the support team for complaints, payment or refund disputes, defective or wrong items, address changes, lost parcels, or whenever you ask to speak to a person. You do not need to repeat your case: the team receives a summary.

## 3. Complaints
We take every complaint seriously. Complaints are reviewed by a team lead, and you will receive a written answer within 5 business days. You can also request an official complaint form.

## 4. Personal data
To look up an order we only need your order number and the email address used for the purchase. Never share passwords or full card numbers in the chat. To access or delete your personal data, contact our support team.
