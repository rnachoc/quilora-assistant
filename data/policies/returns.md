# Returns Policy
<!-- doc_id: RET | updated: 2026-09-01 -->

## 1. Return window
You can return items within 30 calendar days of the delivery date shown in your order tracking. The return request must be created within that period; the parcel can be dropped off up to 5 days later.

## 2. Condition of returned items
Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with all original tags attached and in their original packaging where possible. Shoes must be returned in their original box. Items that do not meet these conditions may be sent back to you.

## 3. Non-returnable items
Final sale items cannot be returned. For hygiene reasons, earrings, underwear and swimwear with a removed hygiene liner cannot be returned either. Personalised items are also excluded.

## 4. How to start a return
Start a return through our assistant or your account page with your order number and email address. You will receive a return reference and a prepaid label by email. Only items that are still within the return window can be selected.

## 5. Return methods and costs
Drop-off at a partner collection point is free for orders shipped to mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands. Home pickup in Spain costs 4.95 EUR, which is deducted from your refund. Returns from other countries are shipped at the customer's expense.

## 6. Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days after our warehouse receives and inspects the return. Original standard shipping costs are refunded only when the entire order is returned within 14 days of delivery. Customer service cannot approve or speed up refunds outside this process.

## 7. Defective or wrong items
If an item arrives defective, damaged or different from what you ordered, contact our support team before starting a return. We will review the case, cover the return shipping and offer a replacement or refund once the claim is confirmed.
