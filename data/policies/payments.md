# Payments Policy
<!-- doc_id: PAY | updated: 2026-09-01 | author: Ignacio Rodríguez Caro -->

## 1. Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, PayPal, Bizum, Apple Pay and Google Pay. All prices are in euros and include VAT for deliveries within the European Union.

## 2. When you are charged
Your payment method is charged when you place the order. If an order is cancelled before shipping, the full amount is returned to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## 3. Discount codes and promotions
Promotions and discount codes are published only through our newsletter and on the website banner. Customer service and the assistant cannot create, share or apply discount codes, and codes cannot be applied to orders that have already been placed.

## 4. Invoices
An invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email. If you need an invoice with company details, contact our support team within 30 days of the order date.

## 5. Payment problems
If a payment fails, the order is not placed and no amount is charged. Duplicate charges or charges you do not recognise must be reviewed by our support team.
